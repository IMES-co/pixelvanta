Image Usage Guidelines

These categories are general information, not legal conclusions. Check the specific record, licence, rights holder's authority and applicable law.

Personal use

Private viewing or sharing may be allowed by a licence or applicable exception; public reposting can be different.

Educational use

Some educational uses may be covered by statutory exceptions or licences, depending on purpose, institution, amount and jurisdiction.

Editorial use

News reporting, criticism, review and commentary may have specific legal treatment. Context and local law matter.

Commercial use

Advertising, product promotion and resale often require an appropriate commercial licence unless another legal basis applies.

Reproduction and redistribution

Copying, reposting, sharing or distributing copies may engage exclusive rights. Check the rights record and legal exceptions.

Modification

Cropping, adapting, remixing or creating derivatives may require permission, subject to applicable law and licence terms.

AI training and dataset use

Use of images in AI training or datasets raises fact-specific questions involving copyright, licences, contracts, privacy and jurisdiction. Check applicable law and obtain permissions where required.
