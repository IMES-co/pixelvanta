Permission should be obtained from the legitimate rights holder when required. Statutory exceptions and limitations under applicable copyright law remain applicable; PIXELVANTA does not decide whether a particular use is lawful.

Report alleged infringement

  1. Identify the public PIXELVANTA record, if available.
  2. Provide the exact URL of the allegedly infringing material.
  3. Explain the work, rights basis and alleged unauthorised use.
  4. Provide good-faith confirmation and an electronic signature.
  5. Submit evidence that you are authorised to act for the rights holder.

Reports are reviewed by an authorised person. A report does not automatically send a legal notice, remove content from third-party services, or guarantee a particular outcome. Knowingly false reports may have legal consequences.
